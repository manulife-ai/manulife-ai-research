# Careers

We're always interested in connecting with researchers and engineers who want to work on applied AI problems in insurance and financial services — spanning underwriting, claims, distribution, and virtual assistants, but not limited to it. Our teams also work on reinforcement learning-based budget optimization for harness engineering, multi-token prediction, novel responsible AI approaches in industry, scalability of RAG in industrial settings, reasoning on complex long-horizon tasks over application manuals, novel secor ranking deep learning models for portfolio optmization, selecting optimal vision language model based on task specific constraints, and others.
