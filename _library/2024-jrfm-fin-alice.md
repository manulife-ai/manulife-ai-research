Fin-ALICE (Artificial Linguistic Intelligence Causal Econometrics) presents a novel framework that combines natural language processing with causal econometric methods for financial market analysis.

**Key Contributions:**
- Integration of linguistic intelligence with causal econometric models
- Application to financial market prediction and analysis
- Framework for extracting causal relationships from financial text data
