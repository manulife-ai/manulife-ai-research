This research presents a framework for improving financial market analysis and prediction by leveraging emotion corpora and news co-occurrence networks.

**Key Contributions:**
- Construction and application of emotion corpora for financial text analysis
- News co-occurrence network for capturing relationships between financial events
- Combined framework for enhanced market prediction
