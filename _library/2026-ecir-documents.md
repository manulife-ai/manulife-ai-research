This paper presents methods for understanding multi-structured financial documents using Large Language Models. Financial reports often contain a complex mix of text, tables, charts, and figures that pose challenges for automated processing.

**Key Contributions:**
- Pipeline for extracting and processing text, tables, and figures from financial report PDFs
- Chart-to-table conversion for structured understanding of visual data
- RAG-based question answering over multi-modal financial documents
- Evaluation on real-world financial reports
