This research applies natural language processing techniques to social media data to gain insights into patient-reported health outcomes of post-COVID-19 condition (long COVID). The work was conducted in collaboration with the Vector Institute.

**Key Contributions:**
- NLP pipeline for extracting health outcome mentions from social media posts
- Analysis of patient-reported symptoms and experiences with long COVID
- Insights into health outcomes that may be underrepresented in clinical data
