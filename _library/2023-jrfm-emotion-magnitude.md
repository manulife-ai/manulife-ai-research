This research examines how the magnitude of emotion expressed in financial news and media influences market dynamics during supply chain disruption events.

**Key Contributions:**
- Analysis of emotion magnitude as a factor in market response to supply chain events
- Quantification of emotional intensity in financial news coverage
- Empirical evidence linking emotion magnitude to market price movements
